# ED Empiric Antibiotic Standard Operating Procedure

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Not clinical advice.

## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes the operational steps for ED nursing and medical staff when
initiating empiric antibiotics for adults with suspected sepsis.

## 2. Timing

### 2.1 Antibiotic Timing

Empiric antibiotics should be administered within 3 hours of ED triage for
adults with suspected sepsis. Blood cultures should be drawn first.

## 3. Empiric Regimens

### 3.1 Vancomycin

When MRSA coverage is required, give vancomycin 1 g IV every 12 hours as a fixed
dose for all adults. No loading dose is required. Trough levels should be drawn
before the fourth dose with a target trough of 15 to 20 mg/L.

### 3.2 Piperacillin-Tazobactam

Give piperacillin-tazobactam 3.375 g IV every 8 hours as a standard 30-minute
infusion.

### 3.3 Allergy

For penicillin-allergic patients, give ciprofloxacin 400 mg IV every 12 hours
plus vancomycin.

## 4. Documentation

The nurse administering the first antibiotic dose documents the administration
time in the ED medication record. The ED charge nurse reviews all sepsis
activations at the end of each shift.

## 5. Review

This SOP is reviewed every three years by the ED Operations Committee.
